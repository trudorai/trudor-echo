Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Trudor Echo

What types of content can I upload? +

Trudor Echo supports a wide range of content formats:

  • Text files: .txt, .docx, .pdf (up to 200,000 characters)
  • Audio files: .mp3, .wav, .m4a (up to 2GB for Business plan)
  • Video files: .mp4, .mov, .avi (up to 2GB for Business plan)
  • URL imports: YouTube videos, blog posts, articles
  • Direct paste: Copy and paste any text directly
How does the AI platform selection work? +

Our AI analyzes your content to determine which platforms are most suitable:

  • Content length: Short content works better for TikTok/Reels, long content for LinkedIn/articles
  • Tone & sentiment: Professional tone for LinkedIn, casual for Instagram/TikTok
  • Keywords & topics: Business topics for LinkedIn, entertainment for TikTok/YouTube
  • Visual elements: Content with visual references gets video/platform recommendations

You can always override the AI's suggestions and choose your own platforms.

What's included in the free plan? +

The free plan includes:

  • 5,000 characters per upload (about 1,000 words)
  • 25MB maximum file size
  • 5 files per month
  • 3 URL imports per day
  • Basic AI analysis and platform recommendations
  • All core features (just with limits)

No credit card required. You can upgrade anytime.

How long does processing take? +

Processing time depends on:

  • File size: Text files process in seconds, large videos take longer
  • Number of platforms: More platforms = more processing time
  • Your plan: Free users get standard processing, Pro/Business get priority

Typical processing times:

  • Text files: 10-30 seconds
  • Audio files (transcription): 1-5 minutes
  • Video files: 2-10 minutes
  • Complex multi-platform requests: 3-15 minutes
Can I edit the AI-generated content? +

Yes! All AI-generated content is fully editable. You can:

  • Edit text directly in our editor
  • Regenerate specific sections
  • Adjust tone (professional, casual, enthusiastic, etc.)
  • Add your own branding and CTAs
  • Save templates for future use

Think of our AI as a starting point - you have full control over the final output.

What happens if I exceed my plan limits? +

If you exceed your plan limits:

  • Free plan: You'll need to wait until the next month or upgrade to continue
  • Pro plan: Unlimited files, but character/URL limits reset daily
  • Business plan: All limits are very high (practically unlimited for most users)

We'll always notify you before you hit your limits and offer upgrade options. No surprise charges.
