🔄 User Flow Test - Anonymous Visitor

Test if someone can easily understand the product, see value, find legal info, and navigate without frustration.

Step 1: First Impression (10 seconds)

⏱️ Goal: Understand product in under 10 seconds

Task: Visit Homepage

Open homepage in new tab

Quickly scan the page. Can you tell:

  • ✅ What the product does?
  • ✅ Who it's for?
  • ✅ The main benefit?
  • ✅ How to get started?

Did you understand the product in 10 seconds?

Step 2: See Value (30 seconds)

⏱️ Goal: See clear value proposition

Task: Explore Value Propositions

Look for:

  • ✅ Specific benefits (save time, increase output)
  • ✅ Social proof (testimonials, metrics)
  • ✅ Visual demonstrations
  • ✅ Clear "How It Works"

Is the value clear and compelling?

Step 3: Try Demo (1 minute)

⏱️ Goal: Try free demo without friction

Task: Use the Free Demo

Scroll to upload section and:

  1. Find the demo content (pre-filled)
  2. Select some platforms
  3. Click "Generate Free Demo"
  4. See AI outputs

Was the demo easy to try?

Step 4: Find Pricing (30 seconds)

⏱️ Goal: Find pricing within 2 clicks

Task: Locate Pricing Information

Starting from homepage:

  1. Find pricing link in navigation
  2. Click to pricing page
  3. Understand plan differences
  4. See clear pricing (Free, Pro, Business)

Was pricing easy to find and understand?

Step 5: Legal Info (30 seconds)

⏱️ Goal: Find legal pages easily

Task: Access Legal Information

Find and open:

  • ✅ Privacy Policy
  • ✅ Terms of Service
  • ✅ Contact information

Were legal pages accessible?

Step 6: Trust & Security (30 seconds)

⏱️ Goal: Feel confident about security

Task: Identify Trust Signals

Look for:

  • ✅ Security mentions near upload
  • ✅ Data privacy assurances
  • ✅ Contact/support options
  • ✅ Professional design

Do you feel the site is trustworthy?
