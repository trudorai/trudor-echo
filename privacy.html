Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Trudor Echo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password.
  • Content: The text, files, and URLs you upload for content repurposing.
  • Payment Information: When you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect payment information through our secure payment processor (Stripe).
  • Communications: If you contact us, we collect your messages and contact information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process your transactions and send related information
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and support messages
  • Respond to your comments and questions
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With Service Providers: To help us operate our services (e.g., payment processing, email delivery).
  • For Legal Reasons: If required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@trudor.ai.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to analyze trends, administer the website, and track users' movements around the site.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@trudor.ai
Address: Trudor Echo, 123 Innovation Drive, Toronto, ON M5H 2N2, Canada
